HOW TO FIND CAVES NOW:
1. Generate new chunks (caves won't appear in existing chunks)
2. Look for holes/openings in the terrain at surface level
3. Check Unity Console for cave coordinates:
   - Look for "🕳️ SURFACE CAVE FOUND at (x,y,z)"
   - Look for "🚪 CAVE ENTRANCE GENERATED at (x,y,z)"
4. Dig down at those coordinates to find cave systems
